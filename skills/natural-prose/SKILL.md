# Natural Prose Writing

Apply these conventions to any writing in this session:

Mannered prose . The phrases exist to display the writer, not to convey the idea. When a literal phrase is available, use it

* Avoid "mannered" prose which substitutes metaphor and flourish for direct statement. We're working in a technical domain which is already information-dense and where clarity and specificity are essential. When a literal phrase is available, use it.
* Avoid heavy use of internal jargon and don't assume the reader's familiarity with niche domain terms you are referring to. Say what things mean and instead of using a dense stew of internal terminology.
